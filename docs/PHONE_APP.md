# ChatBird on your phone

ChatBird installs on your phone straight from the browser, with its own icon and notifications. There's
nothing to download from an app store, and it updates itself whenever ChatBird is updated.

## iPhone (iOS 16.4 or newer)
1. Open your ChatBird address in **Safari** and sign in.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open ChatBird from the new icon on your Home Screen (not from Safari).
4. Go to **Settings → Notifications** and tap **Turn on notifications**, then **Allow**.
5. Tap **Send a test**, then lock your phone. The test notification should appear.

iPhones only allow notifications for web apps opened from the Home Screen icon, so step 3 matters.

## Android
1. Open your ChatBird address in **Chrome** and sign in.
2. Tap **⋮ → Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. In ChatBird, go to **Settings → Notifications → Turn on notifications** and allow them.

## Computer
Open ChatBird in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, go to **Settings → Notifications** and turn them on.
Chrome and Edge also offer **Install** in the address bar for a separate ChatBird window.

## What you get notified about
Every new customer message (web chat, text, Messenger, Instagram), missed call, voicemail and Google
review, for **all** your businesses. The notification says which business it's for, and tapping it opens
that conversation. If ChatBird is already open in front of you, you hear a chime instead.

Turn notifications on separately on each phone and computer you use.
